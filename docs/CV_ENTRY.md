### Factors Influencing Online Purchase Intention Among Pakistani Consumers
*Final-Year Quantitative Research Project | GitHub Portfolio*

- Investigated factors associated with online purchase intention among Pakistani consumers, focusing on perceived risk, payment trust, delivery reliability, and related e-commerce factors.
- Designed a structured primary-data study and developed a reproducible analysis workflow covering data cleaning, correlation analysis, regression modeling, visualization, and interpretation.
- Translated empirical findings into practical recommendations concerning payment trust, transparent returns, delivery reliability, and shipment tracking.
